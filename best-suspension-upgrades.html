Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between JL and JLU suspension upgrades?
The JL is the 2-door Wrangler, the JLU is the 4-door Unlimited. Most lift kits list both — the geometry is similar but wheelbase-specific spacers or length-matched shocks are sometimes different. Always verify the kit specifies your configuration. All products on this page cover JL and JLU unless noted.
Do these kits work on JK and JKU Wranglers (2007–2018)?
The JK/JKU (2007–2018) use different suspension geometry than the JL/JLU (2018+). Products listed here are verified for JL/JLU. Many brands have separate JK/JKU versions available at Bull Strap — search your year/model directly.
What tire size can I run with a 3-inch lift on a JL or JLU?
A 3-inch lift on a stock-width Wrangler JL/JLU will typically clear 33-inch tires with minimal trimming. 35-inch tires usually require 3.5–4.5 inches of lift plus some fender trimming. The Rock Krawler and Zone kits on this page are sized appropriately for their recommended tire ranges.
