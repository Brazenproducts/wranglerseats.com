Jeep Gladiator Seat Cover Options in 2026 — What Fits, What Doesn't

A lot of Gladiator owners try to run Wrangler JL seat covers and run into problems. The Gladiator and JL share a platform, but the seat configuration is different — especially in the rear crew cab area. Here's what you need to know before buying.

Gladiator vs JL Front Seats

The front seats are close but not identical to the JL. The headrest posts are the same diameter, but the seat back contour is slightly different on the Gladiator due to the different door angle. Some JL front covers fit, most don't sit flush at the lower corners.

Rear Seat Differences

This is where Gladiator is completely different. The crew cab rear bench folds differently, the seat back hinge point is different, and the floor clearance is different. No JL rear cover fits a Gladiator rear — don't try.

What to Look For

  • Covers explicitly labeled "Jeep Gladiator JT" — not "JL/Gladiator compatible"
  • Rear patterns that account for the 60/40 fold on the Gladiator bench
  • SRS airbag-safe construction — same requirement as the JL
  • MOLLE compatibility if you run trail gear

Color and Material

The Gladiator gets more sun than most Jeeps due to the open design. UV-stabilized fabric is not optional — standard polyester bleaches out within one summer of Southwest exposure. Nylon with UV-stabilized thread is the minimum spec worth buying.
